## Fee Clarification: Exit Load
### Reusable Support Snippet

  • Exit load is a charge levied only when units are redeemed before the fund‑specified minimum holding period; if the investment is held longer than that period, no exit load is applied.
  • The charge is calculated as a percentage of the total redemption value (principal + gains) and is deducted from the amount paid out, not separately from the principal.
  • Exit‑load percentages differ by scheme type (e.g., equity, debt, hybrid) and by the length of the holding period; they are not uniform across all mutual funds.
  • For systematic investment plans (SIPs), each instalment starts its own holding‑period clock, so an early redemption of a later instalment may attract an exit load even if earlier instalments have matured.
  • The exit‑load amount is subject to Goods and Services Tax (GST) at the prevailing rate, which is added to the charge before deduction
