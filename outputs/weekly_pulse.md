## Fee Clarification: Exit Load
### Reusable Support Snippet

  • Exit Load is a statutory charge levied by the Asset Management Company (AMC) if mutual fund units are redeemed before a mandated holding period.
  • For Systematic Investment Plans (SIPs), each monthly installment carries its own individual holding clock measured from its unique allotment date.
  • Redemptions strictly adhere to First-In, First-Out (FIFO) accounting, liquidating oldest purchased units first.
  • Exit load is credited directly back to the fund scheme to safeguard continuing long-term investors, not retained as platform brokerage.
  • Applicable Goods and Services Tax (GST) of 18% is levied on the exit load amount as required by statutory tax regulations.
  • Certain categories (e.g., overnight funds, liquid funds held past 7 days, or equity funds held past 365 days) carry nil exit load once their holding period is completed.

**Official Sources:**
  - https://www.sebi.gov.in/legal/circulars/jun-2009/exit-load-mutual-funds_3555.html

## Fee Clarification: Brokerage
### Reusable Support Snippet

  • Brokerage is the fee charged by the broker for executing each purchase or sale of mutual fund units and is calculated per transaction segment, not as a single flat charge on the total investment.
  • The fee is disclosed in the transaction statement; GST of 18% is applied on the brokerage amount and shown separately, so there are no hidden taxes beyond the GST.
  • Brokerage rates are determined by the broker and may be revised periodically; any change is communicated through official notices and is not applied without prior disclosure.
  • Brokerage is distinct from exit load: brokerage is levied at the time of trade, whereas exit load is a redemption charge applied after a specified holding period.
  • For SIPs, each installment has its own holding period; if an installment is redeemed before its period ends, the applicable exit load is charged only on that installment.
  • GST at 18% is also levied on the exit load amount, and this tax is reflected in the redemption statement.

## Fee Clarification: Exit Load
### Reusable Support Snippet

  • Exit load is a charge applied only when units are redeemed before the fund’s specified minimum holding period; it is not levied on every redemption after that period.
  • The applicable percentage varies by scheme type (e.g., equity, debt, hybrid) and is disclosed in the scheme’s offer document; it is not a uniform rate across all categories.

  • GST at the prevailing rate is applicable on the exit load amount, and the tax is added to the load before deduction from the redemption proceeds.
